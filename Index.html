💡 Microplastic Safety Threshold Reference Guide

  • 🟢 Safe Zone (Raw Score < 20): permissible limit for human consumption. Water has negligible microplastic index that human metabolism can naturally filter out without harm.
  • 🟡 Moderate / Warning Zone (Raw Score 20 - 50): Elevated concentration. Particle scattering detected above safety levels. Standard micro-filtration or purification membranes are highly recommended before use.
  • 🔴 Danger Zone (Raw Score > 50): High contamination level. Toxic particle densities present that can cause cell leaching or organ blockages. Strictly unsafe for drinking purposes.
