💡 Microplastic Safety Limits Reference Guide

  • 🟢 Safe Zone (Raw Score < 20): Normal and safe limit for human body. Water has negligible microplastics that human digestion system can naturally filter out without harm.
  • 🟡 Moderate Warning Zone (Raw Score 20 - 50): High concentration detected. Light scattering is high. Pre-filtration and boiling are recommended before using this water.
  • 🔴 Critical Danger Zone (Raw Score > 50): Severe plastic contamination! Light is blocked too much by toxic synthetic plastic elements. Strictly unsafe for drinking.
